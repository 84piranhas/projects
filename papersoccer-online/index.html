Paper soccer — online

  • The ball starts on the centre point. P1 attacks P2's goal and P2 attacks P1's goal. Your own goal is always shown at the bottom of your screen.
  • A move draws a line from the ball to one of its 8 neighbouring points. A line can never be drawn twice, and you can't move along the border. Diagonals may cross.
  • Bounce: landing on a point that was already touched (border, post, corner or the end of an earlier line) gives you another move.
  • Goal in the opponent's goal wins; an own goal loses. If you must move and can't, you lose.
  • Undo asks your opponent to take back your last move. Resign gives the game to your opponent.
  • Chess clock (optional): 2:00 each, +4 s after each of your turns. It pauses while a player is disconnected.

Controls

  • Tap or click a highlighted point. Filled targets give an extra move.
  • Keys (relative to your screen): QWE / AD / ZXC, numpad 7–9, or arrows (hold Shift and press two arrows for a diagonal).
